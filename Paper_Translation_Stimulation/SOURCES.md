# 论文来源与署名 / Paper sources and credits

本文适用于本目录中的英文论文、中文全文与选页，以及项目文档展示的 PDF 摘录和页图。每篇材料沿用其下列来源及权利说明。

These credits cover the English papers, Chinese translations and annotated pages in this directory, along with excerpts and page images displayed in the project documentation. Each work has its own terms below.

## Open-Canopy

**Fajwel Fogel, Yohann Perron, Nikola Besic, Laurent Saint-André, Agnès Pellissier-Tanon, Martin Schwartz, Thomas Boudras, Ibrahim Fayad, Alexandre d'Aspremont, Loic Landrieu and Philippe Ciais.** *Open-Canopy: Towards Very High Resolution Forest Monitoring*. arXiv:2407.09392v4，2024 年 12 月 11 日。会议论文发表于 CVPR 2025。项目采用含补充材料的 25 页作者版，依照该版本标明的 CC BY 4.0 许可分发。[作者版与许可入口 / Version and license record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.09392v4) · [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)

英文原稿随附于测试目录。第 2、3、4、7、9 轮的中文 PDF、译文清单、原译对照页，以及项目文档中的 Open-Canopy 样张和截取图均以该作者版为来源。项目完成中文翻译、图内标签释义、版式调整、裁切或重新组合，并在精读版本中加入圈划、示意图和阅读手记。保留原论文内图表、影像的来源与署名。附录列出的 Etalab 开放许可 2.0 适用于论文所述数据集。单独取得数据或重用第三方材料时，按相应来源给出的署名与许可办理。[英文原稿](CVPR2025-Open-Canopy-arXiv-v4.pdf) · [论证精读手稿](round-09-Open-Canopy/Open-Canopy-CVPR2025-论证精读手稿.pdf)

The 25-page author manuscript, including its supplement, is distributed under CC BY 4.0. Rounds 2, 3, 4, 7 and 9 and the Open-Canopy examples in the documentation use that version. Project changes include Chinese translation, explanations of figure labels, layout, cropping and assembly. The annotated editions add handwritten notes, marks and sketches. The authors receive credit for the paper. Figure and imagery credits accompanying the source material still apply. The Etalab Open Licence 2.0 cited in the appendix concerns the dataset. [Author manuscript](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.09392v4) · [Paper license](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)
